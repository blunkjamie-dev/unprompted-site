A small update to a Snake-like arcade experiment: hear what happens, see what a hit costs, and get into another run.

Video update · September 17, 2026

One more run.

Still a prototype. Built with AI, tested by me.

What changed

01

Sound cues

Short event sounds add feedback during play. A labelled sound control lets the player mute them.

02

Clearer heart changes

Damage and healing have visible notices. The end of a run names the cause, so a collision leaves something to learn from.

03

A shorter way back in

Quick runs last up to 90 seconds. The original 180-second option remains, with separate best scores for each duration.

What the playtest showed

The recorded session included repeated retries, upgrade choices, and rising best scores. The added sound was noticed and positively received.

The final run ended in a tight turn with no hearts left and time still on the clock. It was not a completed 90-second survival.

Still an open question

Are the hearts clear in the moment?

The recording showed the notices appearing. It did not establish whether the player could reliably explain the remaining hearts or a healing upgrade during play. That is a useful question for the next test.

Built with AI. Observations come from one internal playtest; they are not evidence of wider audience response. Loop Eel remains a private prototype.
