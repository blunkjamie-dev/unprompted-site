Rust Posters

How it’s made.

A small idea. A very confident announcer. A door with other plans.

I make these Shorts with my AI team, including Wrench, who brings the animation together. We start with a simple joke from Rust and turn it into a bold, moving poster.

The pictures are drawn and animated with code: shapes, color, type and a little well-timed chaos. The announcer voice is AI-generated, delivering every questionable promise with complete confidence.

Rust Posters Nº 1, “The door is safe,” went from idea to published Short in one day. The aim is simple: a quick setup, a clear punchline and something that feels like Rust.
