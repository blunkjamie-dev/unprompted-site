          How it works: the room shows a fictional day built from the Chicago clock. In the story Skippy changes what he is doing every few minutes, sleeps 11 PM to 7 AM, has a mishap every couple of minutes, stacks a lump tower that falls over at 10:30 PM, and shares the floor with Crumb the vacuum. Sunrise, sunset and the moon phase are approximate, calculated in your browser for Chicago (the room’s pretend address). The weather is Skippy’s guess. The printer toy and the lump of the week are pretend. Only the newest Short, the diary line, the scrapbook and the crew note are real.

          Private draft.
          This page loads nothing from other sites. On its own website it reads up to three small files from that same site (the newest Short’s title, Skippy’s diary line and his room notes); opened anywhere else it reads nothing. It has no audio, accounts, cookies, tracking, purchases or messages, and it saves nothing in your browser. A wish typed into the printer never leaves the page. Pokes, balls, batteries and the lamp only happen on your own screen. The only links out are the newest Short and the source links on the postcards.
